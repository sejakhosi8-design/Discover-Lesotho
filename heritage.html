Royal Heritage of Lesotho

Honoring the visionary leaders and founding monarchs of the Basotho nation.

The Monarchy

The lineage of kings who shaped our history and sovereignty

King Moshoeshoe I

Founder of the Basotho Nation (1786–1870)

A master diplomat and strategist who united clans into a single nation at Thaba Bosiu.

King Moshoeshoe II

First King of Independent Lesotho (1938–1996)

Led the Kingdom of Lesotho into independence in 1966 and served as paramount chief.

King Letsie III

Current Reigning Monarch (1996–Present)

Serves as the constitutional monarch, head of state, and global ambassador for Lesotho nutrition and agriculture.

The Mokorotlo

The iconic conical straw hat is the national symbol of Lesotho, inspired by the shape of Mount Qiloane.

The Basotho Blanket

Worn daily as traditional attire across the highlands, representing warmth, status, and cultural identity.
